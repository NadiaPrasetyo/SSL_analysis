📊 Understanding the Metrics

Represents: The count of sequences this representative accounts for (1 = itself, plus any sequences merged with it due to >90% identity)
Coverage %: (Sequences Represented / Total Original Sequences) × 100
Example: If a representative accounts for 25 sequences out of 1000 total, its coverage is 2.5%
⭐ = Always-Keep Reference Sequence — Retained regardless of redundancy
